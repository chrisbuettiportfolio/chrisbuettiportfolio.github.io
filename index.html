Story

CompanyLionize RoleFounder & lead engineer OutcomeAcquired

The Instagram bot was a side project that got out of hand. The company that came after it was the real work.

After the press cycle, brands kept asking the same question: could the matching and outreach be done for them? I started Lionize to answer it, an AI driven SaaS platform that found the right creators, scored them, and ran campaigns end to end. I wrote the first prototype myself and stayed the lead engineer as it grew into a real product with paying customers.

I raised capital, hired and led the team, and owned the systems underneath it: ranking and recommendation models, large scale data ingestion, and the automation pipelines that made campaigns run without a human in the loop. Lionize was acquired, and I stayed through the transition.
